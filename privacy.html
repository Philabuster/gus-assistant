Gus Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

What Gus is

Gus is a personal assistant that runs on the owner's own Mac. It is not a public service. Only the owner and people the owner invites to the household assistant can use it.

Google data Gus accesses

Gus uses Google OAuth to read and manage events in the owner's Google Calendar. It requests only calendar access. Gus reads calendar events to answer questions and prepare schedule summaries, and it creates or changes events only after the owner approves each change.

How the data is stored and used

  • Google access tokens are stored by a local credential gateway (OneCLI) on the owner's Mac. They are injected into requests at runtime and are not given to the assistant itself.
  • Calendar content is processed by Anthropic's Claude service to produce answers and summaries. Anthropic processes this content under its own terms.
  • Gus does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

Retention and deletion

Calendar content is kept only in the owner's local assistant memory. The owner can revoke Gus's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops Gus from accessing the calendar.

Limited Use

Gus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
